# sizbiz for business

> **Value for people. Growth for business. Benefit nearby.**

## What sizbiz is

sizbiz is a digital platform for local offers. It helps a business prepare and
show a campaign to buyers, approve the terms, and track the journey from coupon
purchase to use.

## Partner value

- a dedicated business and offer card;
- help preparing clear campaign terms;
- approval before publication;
- a partner cabinet for offers and staff;
- QR or PIN coupon acceptance;
- available sales and redemption statistics;
- a feedback and issue-resolution path.

## Launch process

1. Understand your business and goal.
2. Define a sustainable offer.
3. Prepare the card and conditions.
4. Review and approve publication.
5. Train the responsible employee to redeem coupons.
6. Launch and observe results.

## What we need from you

- accurate business information;
- final price, validity, and restrictions;
- current images when available;
- an authorized approver;
- staff readiness to accept published coupons.

## Important principle

A good offer must be clear to the buyer and sustainable for the business.
sizbiz does not guarantee a specific sales volume; results depend on the terms,
demand, and service quality.

## Next step

Prepare your business name, contacts, locations, and an offer idea. The sizbiz
team will help turn it into an approved launch scenario.
