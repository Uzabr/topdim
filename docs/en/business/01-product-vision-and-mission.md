# sizbiz vision, mission, and social benefit

## Motto

> **Value for people. Growth for business. Benefit nearby.**

The motto represents three sides of the product: buyers receive clear value,
entrepreneurs gain an additional acquisition channel and measurable demand,
and attention and spending remain closer to the local community.

## Why sizbiz exists

People struggle to find genuinely useful nearby offers, compare terms, and
understand how to use a discount. Small and medium businesses struggle to
attract new customers consistently, measure a campaign, and build repeat
relationships.

sizbiz is being built to make this connection simpler and more transparent:
help a person find, buy, and use a valuable offer, while helping a business
prepare it, reach the right audience, and observe the result.

## Mission

**Make valuable local-business offers more accessible to people and help
entrepreneurs build clear, measurable customer relationships.**

## Vision

sizbiz aims to become a familiar digital meeting point for people and local
businesses in Uzbekistan: a place where a person can confidently find value
and an entrepreneur has an accessible tool for growth.

Uzbekistan is the first market and primary product context. Expansion to other
countries is a possible future direction, not a claim of current presence.

## Value for stakeholders

### People

- easier discovery of current offers;
- clearer prices, discounts, validity periods, and conditions;
- convenient storage and presentation of purchased QR/PIN coupons;
- paths for reviews, questions, complaints, and refunds;
- more opportunities to try nearby goods and services.

### Entrepreneurs

- an additional digital storefront;
- a managed offer-preparation and approval process;
- trackable coupon sales and redemptions;
- owner-managed partner staff and cashiers;
- feedback that can improve an offer.

### Society

- greater visibility for local small and medium businesses;
- more accessible digital tools for entrepreneurs;
- more transparent campaign terms and responsibilities;
- a shift toward measurable rather than improvised promotion;
- better awareness of useful nearby goods and services.

These are **impact intentions**, not claims of already proven outcomes.
Quantitative social impact must be supported by analytics and research.

## Principles

1. **Honest value.** Terms, price, and limits are clear before purchase.
2. **Benefit for both sides.** An offer should not depend on harming either
   buyer or partner.
3. **Local focus.** Solve real Uzbekistan market needs first.
4. **Simplicity.** Users should not need to understand internal architecture.
5. **Measurability.** Sales, redemptions, and feedback inform decisions.
6. **Accountability.** Complaints, refunds, moderation, and audit are product
   responsibilities.
7. **Accessibility.** Web and Telegram lower barriers for different audiences.

## Mission metrics

Once analytics definitions are approved, measure:

- active local partners and published offers;
- successful coupon-use rate;
- repeat purchases and retention;
- time from partner application to publication;
- successfully resolved support and refund cases;
- buyer and partner satisfaction;
- geographic and category coverage.

No approved targets are present in the current documentation. They must not
become public promises without a business decision.
