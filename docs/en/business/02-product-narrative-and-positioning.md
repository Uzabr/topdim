# Product narrative and positioning

## One sentence

**sizbiz is an offer platform that helps people discover and use local-business
offers while helping entrepreneurs attract customers and observe campaign
results.**

## Product story

Useful offers are often scattered across social media, chats, and offline ads.
A person must verify the terms, save a post, and explain it at checkout. A
business runs a discount but may not know how many customers it attracted or
whether it was used.

sizbiz connects the process:

1. a business submits a partnership or offer request;
2. the team helps prepare and review the card;
3. the partner approves the terms;
4. a buyer finds and purchases the coupon;
5. partner staff redeem the QR or PIN;
6. both sides receive operation history and a feedback path.

## Product category

Primary category: **marketplace for coupons and local offers**.

Supporting areas:

- partner directory;
- partner cabinet;
- offer-moderation operations;
- Telegram concierge and communication;
- bazaar/shop directory, present in code but with current infrastructure gaps.

## Positioning

For people in Uzbekistan seeking clear nearby value, sizbiz helps discover,
buy, and use a verifiable offer. For local businesses seeking an accessible
acquisition channel, sizbiz provides a managed journey from campaign idea to
sale and redemption.

Unlike fragmented advertising, sizbiz is not only an ad placement. Its value is
the connected process: publication → purchase → use → feedback.

## Value proposition by audience

| Audience | Job | sizbiz value |
|---|---|---|
| Buyer | Find genuine value | Catalog, clear terms, purchase, and profile coupon |
| Merchant | Attract customers | Offer preparation, publication, sales, and statistics |
| Cashier | Verify quickly | QR/PIN redemption in an authorized location |
| Operations | Maintain quality | Statuses, moderation, complaints, refunds, and audit |

## Available today

- registration and login, including Telegram Login;
- catalog, categories, reviews, and questions;
- favorites, cart, checkout, and orders;
- demo payment and purchased-coupon generation;
- QR/PIN redemption;
- partnership and coupon requests with approval;
- partner staff and statistics;
- refunds, complaints, notifications, and admin moderation.

## Not production-ready promises

- real production payment through a named provider;
- guaranteed email/SMS delivery;
- fully production-enabled bazaar directory;
- settlement, payout, or platform-commission cabinet;
- guaranteed sales volume or partner income.

## Tone

Simple, respectful, local, and specific. Do not claim “the best discount” without
evidence or create artificial urgency. Explain conditions and the next step.
